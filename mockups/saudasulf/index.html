A lighter, friendlier SaudaSulf

Every current screen, redrawn with white and soft-grey surfaces, one confident brand colour, rounded 18px cards, bigger tap targets and colour-coded order statuses. It replaces today's brown/terracotta Material 3 theme, which felt heavy and dated. The screens, fields, actions and strings are the app's real ones (from lib/features and app_en.arb); only the look has changed.

After those, two planned sections show the 17 screens the design spec still needs and the app doesn't have yet: chat, notifications, scanning a QR, shop sign-up, paying with a screenshot, adding products and more. They are marked PLANNED, and each names the part of the spec it comes from.

Design system

Everything above is driven by these tokens. For Flutter, build each palette as an explicit ColorScheme (not fromSeed, which would shift the hues), with useMaterial3: true. Put the extra tokens (status colours, the hero gradient, the vivid brand colour) in a ThemeExtension named SaudaColors. Each cell shows light / dark. Every text pair below passes WCAG AA (4.5:1 or more).

Elevation

Cards: 0 1 2 /4% + 0 6 18 -6 /10% of #101828. Primary buttons and the cart bar get a coloured glow (primary at 70%, blur 18–30, y 8–16). In dark mode, shadows become a 1px #232831 border.

Components

  • FilledButton: height 52, radius 14, labelLarge. Variants: primary, tonal (primaryContainer), outline (1.5px outlineVariant, primary text), text, danger text. Small: 40 / 36 high.
  • TextField: filled white, radius 14, 1.5px outlineVariant; focus is a 2px primary border plus a 4px 14% glow. Label sits inside, 12/700. Leading icon in onSurfaceVariant.
  • Search: 48 high, search icon, same border as fields.
  • ChoiceChip: 38 high, full radius. Customer selected = primary; shop selected = onSurface (neutral). Count in a 22px bubble.
  • StatusChip: 26 high, a 6px dot plus a label, from SaudaColors.status.
  • Product card: 2-column grid, 18 radius, an 80px image tile (photo, or a category gradient plus an icon as the fallback), name, unit, price; Add (outlined) becomes a Stepper (primary, 36 high).
  • Cart bar: floats 16 from the edges and 30 from the bottom, 62 high, radius 18, item-count badge in accent. Hidden when the cart is empty.
  • Sticky footer: white, top hairline, total plus the primary action (Cart).
  • Order tracker: 28px step dots joined by a 3px line; done and current are primary, the current one has a 6px halo.
  • Hero card: hero gradient, radius 22, two soft white circles, white display number.
  • NavigationBar: white, 5 tabs, 56×30 pill indicator in primaryContainer, labels 11.5/700.
  • List group: white card, rows of at least 58 high, 40px icon tile (primaryContainer), hairline separators, trailing chevron.
  • Banner: tertiaryContainer, radius 16, warning icon, optional tonal action. Severe (closed, grace, ended) uses errorContainer.
  • Empty state: a 168px tinted circle, a white 96px icon tile with a shadow, accent dots, then headline, body and one button.
  • Bottom sheet: radius 28 top, 40×4 handle. Switch: M3 switch on primary.
