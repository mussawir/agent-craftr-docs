CoAgent · Flutter app · Redesign mockups

One hub for every business on WhatsApp

CoAgent is where a business runs communication, customers, staff, khata and leads from its phone. These screens are a fresh look in the same family as SaudaSulf: the same neutrals and status colours, with its own brand colour (teal and saffron by default). Built screens are redrawn from the real app (lib/features and app_en.arb); PLANNED ones come from the approved plan (Part P and Part B of 2026-11-18-coagent-rename-fulfilment-docs) and the “CoAgent for every trade” proposal, and each names its source. All names, amounts and businesses are sample data.

Design system

Everything above is driven by these tokens. For Flutter, build each palette as an explicit ColorScheme (not fromSeed), with useMaterial3: true. Extra tokens (status colours, the hero gradient, the vivid brand colour, the AI tint) live in a ThemeExtension named CoAgentColors. Each cell shows light / dark. The neutrals and status colours are the same as SaudaSulf, so the two apps read as one family; only the brand and accent change. Default: A · Firoza.

Components

  • FilledButton: height 52, radius 14. Variants: primary, tonal, outline, text, danger. Small 40 / 36 high.
  • TextField: filled white, radius 14, 1.5px outlineVariant; focus is a 2px primary border plus a 4px glow; the label sits inside. Two small fields can share a row (Set up forms).
  • ChoiceChip: 38 high, full radius, count in a 22px bubble. Time chips are 36 high in a 4-column grid.
  • StatusChip: 26 high (22 compact), a 6px dot and a label. The AI pill has a sparkle instead of a dot.
  • AI bubble: tint fill, 1px primary border at 22%, an “AI” label with a sparkle above it. The owner's bubbles are solid primary.
  • Take-over banner: accent container, a Take over button; the same banner also holds Give back to AI.
  • Slot row: time column plus a card with a 4px status-coloured left edge (right edge in RTL).
  • Hours editor: one row per day, an Open switch, one or more time ranges, a +Range button for a lunch break.
  • Proof strip: three 64px tiles (photo, video, voice) on a task.
  • NavigationBar: 3 to 5 tabs from the feature switches; labels use the trade's words (Appointments, Cases, Clients). Warehouse and rider logins get 3 tabs.
  • List group: white card, rows of 54–64, a 40px icon tile, hairline separators, trailing chevron.
  • Set up group: a normal list group under an “Admins only” overline; not rendered for staff.
  • Bottom sheet: radius 28 top, 40×4 handle, used for Invite and Crew edit. Switch: M3 switch on primary.
  • Hero card: hero gradient, radius 22, two soft circles, white display number.
